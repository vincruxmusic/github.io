About VinCrux

Raosaheb Ramteke, known professionally as VinCrux, is a Nagpur-born multi-genre record producer and songwriter who blends melody-forward writing with club-ready sound design.

Spanning electronic, house, hip-hop, alt pop and alt R&B, his catalog is defined by emotional clarity, modern grooves and cinematic textures: music made for both late-night headphones and high-energy rooms.

He began producing in 2018, turning music into both a creative refuge and an adrenaline rush, and released his first single in 2020 during the pandemic. His tracks have since been broadcast on SongdewTV, reaching more than 200 million households. Releasing independently through his own imprint, VinCrux Records, he is on a mission to soundtrack real life, one mood, one memory and one drop at a time.

Questions

Quick answers about VinCrux and his music.

Who is VinCrux?

VinCrux is the stage name of Raosaheb Ramteke, a Nagpur-born record producer and songwriter. He makes electronic, house, hip-hop, alt pop and alt R&B music that pairs melody-forward writing with club-ready sound design.

What is VinCrux’s latest release?

The Somewhere EP, released on 9 October 2026. It has two tracks, “Somewhere” and “Stay, Stay, Stay”, and is streaming on Spotify and YouTube Music.

What kind of music does VinCrux make?

Bright 100–130 BPM grooves with cinematic textures and modern rap and sung hooks. His styles include melodic house, tech house, pop rap and R&B: music made for both late-night headphones and high-energy rooms.

Where is VinCrux from?

VinCrux was born in Nagpur, India.

When did VinCrux start making music?

He began producing in 2018 and released his first single in 2020. His tracks have since been broadcast on SongdewTV, reaching more than 200 million households.

Which record label releases VinCrux’s music?

VinCrux releases his music independently through his own imprint, VinCrux Records.

Where can I listen to and follow VinCrux?

Stream VinCrux on Spotify and YouTube, and follow him on Instagram and TikTok at @vincrux.music, on X at @CruxVin and on Facebook at vincrux.official.
