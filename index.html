VINCRUX
Electronic | House | Hip-Hop | R&B

Raosaheb Ramteke, known professionally as VinCrux, is a Nagpur-born multi-genre record producer and songwriter who blends melody-forward writing with club-ready sound design. Spanning electronic, house, hip-hop, alt pop, and alt R&B, VinCrux has built a catalog defined by emotional clarity, modern grooves, and cinematic textures—music designed for both late-night headphones and high-energy rooms. He began producing in 2018, quickly turning music into both a creative refuge and an adrenaline rush, and released his first single in 2020 during the pandemic. Since then, his reach has continued to grow, with tracks broadcast on SongdewTV, bringing his sound to 200+ million households. Releasing independently through his own imprint, VinCrux Records, he is on a mission to soundtrack real life—one mood, one memory, and one drop at a time.
